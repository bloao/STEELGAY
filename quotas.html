How to read this

  • Balances come from HMRC's Trade Tariff quota service; any line it doesn't return is filled from the Department for Business and Trade daily quota dataset (the row is marked "daily").
  • Quotas run quarterly (Q1 Jul–Sep, Q2 Oct–Dec, Q3 Jan–Mar, Q4 Apr–Jun). Unused volume carries into the next quarter, but not into the next quota year.
  • Run-out dates project the average daily use so far this quarter forward. HMRC allocates first come, first served on the day goods are declared.
  • "Residual" lines cover every origin not named for that category. Ukraine is excluded from the measure. Codes 7217 30 49 and 7217 30 90 left Category 28 on 1 October 2026.
  • Category 1 authorised-use goods have a separate global quota with a 40% per-country cap (not shown here).

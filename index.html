Regulatory regime

Post-1 July 2026 UK steel measure.

Twenty categories of steel that the UK considers capable of domestic production sit under a tariff-rate quota. Within quota, tariff-free treatment applies; outside it, a 50% tariff attaches on covered imports.

Supply routes

How steel physically reaches the UK.

Sea, road, rail, air and inland waterway on the physical side; direct mill, trader, agent, warehouse and marketplace on the commercial side. Each route has a distinct process, document set and quota risk profile.

Scenario tooling

Model an order before you place it.

The order-scenario tool combines commodity code, origin, tonnage and price to surface the applicable quota, likely duty exposure and CBAM position, so risk-adjusted landed cost can be checked before contracting.

Supply routes

Every route steel can take into the UK.

Nine practical routes for supplying steel into Britain — grouped by physical transport mode, customs procedure and commercial channel. Each has its own process, documentary requirements and tariff-quota risk profile.

Physical routes

Sea freight Deep-sea and short-sea vessels — the workhorse for coil, plate, billet, bar and pipe in medium and large lots. → Road / RoRo European road and roll-on/roll-off flows where lead time and flexibility matter more than absolute unit cost. → Rail & intermodal Long-haul rail from Eurasian origins and shorter European rail flows — a middle ground on cost and speed. → Air freight Airfreight for samples, emergency specialty grades and very high-value, light consignments. → Inland waterway Barge and estuary movements for heavy lots between UK seaports and inland or riverine sites. →

Customs procedure

Customs / bonded warehouse HMRC-authorised warehousing with duty and import VAT suspended until release or re-export. →

Commercial channels

Direct mill purchase Predictable volume purchased directly from the mill on an agreed specification — best pricing, most responsibility. → Trader or agent Smaller lots and mixed grades through an intermediary or UK stockholder — convenient, with importer-of-record nuance. → Online marketplace B2B platforms for supplier discovery, price comparison and smaller or spot purchases. →

Physical routes

Sea freight Deep-sea and short-sea vessels — the workhorse for coil, plate, billet, bar and pipe in medium and large lots. → Road / RoRo European road and roll-on/roll-off flows where lead time and flexibility matter more than absolute unit cost. → Rail & intermodal Long-haul rail from Eurasian origins and shorter European rail flows — a middle ground on cost and speed. → Air freight Airfreight for samples, emergency specialty grades and very high-value, light consignments. → Inland waterway Barge and estuary movements for heavy lots between UK seaports and inland or riverine sites. →

Customs procedure

Customs / bonded warehouse HMRC-authorised warehousing with duty and import VAT suspended until release or re-export. →

Commercial channels

Direct mill purchase Predictable volume purchased directly from the mill on an agreed specification — best pricing, most responsibility. → Trader or agent Smaller lots and mixed grades through an intermediary or UK stockholder — convenient, with importer-of-record nuance. → Online marketplace B2B platforms for supplier discovery, price comparison and smaller or spot purchases. →

Procurement intelligence · System design

The plan — from public trade data to a buy/wait recommendation.

A seven-stage pipeline that fuses public trade data, supplier intelligence, live market inputs and the company’s own purchasing and inventory data to answer, for every steel product: where it is coming from, who is likely supplying it, what the true landed cost is, where the market is heading, when to buy, how much to buy, and which supplier or origin offers the best risk-adjusted procurement cost.

Confirmed Inferred Forecast Internal company
  1. 01

    Stage 1

    Trade mapping

    Establish, from HMRC records, what steel is actually entering the UK, from where, on what routes and to whom.

    Inputs

    • Confirmed HMRC imports 2024–2026 — commodity code, origin country, dispatch country, transport mode, tonnes, statistical value.
    • Confirmed HMRC control files — translate commodity codes into product descriptions.
    • Confirmed HMRC importer details — UK importer name, address/postcode, commodity codes imported, activity across 2024, 2025 and 2026.
    • Confirmed Customs declaration statistics — major UK entry locations and mode-of-transport context.

    Outputs

    • Steel products entering the UK.
    • Countries of origin and countries of dispatch.
    • Import volumes and values.
    • Transport modes.
    • UK companies importing the products.
  2. 02

    Stage 2

    Supplier discovery

    Move from “country” to “mill”. Assemble a supplier universe and infer likely supplier → UK importer relationships with a defensible evidence score.

    Inputs

    • Confirmed Origin-country export statistics — France, Germany, Turkey, other significant steel-producing / exporting countries.
    • Confirmed Supplier & mill websites, product catalogues — products manufactured, grades, dimensions, export markets.
    • Inferred Shipping & trade databases — Volza, Trademo, bill-of-lading or shipment sources where available.
    • Inferred Market intelligence — Kallanish, supplier network information, NSA and relevant industry-association information.
    • Inferred Route validation datasets — RORO0901, PORT0203, PORT0204, PORT0205. Supporting evidence only, never the main supplier identifier.

    Outputs

    • Foreign supplier universe by product and country.
    • Candidate supplier → UK importer relationships.
    • Evidence score for each candidate link.
    • Confidence score for the shortlist.
    • Likely supplier shortlist per product / importer.

    Route information is supporting evidence, never the primary basis for identifying a supplier. Direct mill–importer signals (contracts, catalogues, invoices) outrank inferred flows.

  3. 03

    Stage 3

    Landed cost & risk engine

    Convert every candidate supplier and route into a true landed / replacement cost per tonne, with quota, duty, FX and CBAM risk priced in.

    Inputs

    • Confirmed Supplier or trader quotes.
    • Inferred Known transport costs.
      • EU merchant bar — approx. £65–75 per tonne delivered, depending on depot.
      • Turkish hollow section — sea freight £80–90/t · insurance £5–10/t · terminal handling / customs £20/t · docks to depot £20/t.
    • Confirmed UK steel quota data — category, quota number, quarterly allocation, utilisation, remaining quota, exhaustion risk, potential duty.
    • Confirmed Currency — GBP/EUR, GBP/USD.
    • Confirmed Other market inputs — scrap prices, iron ore & raw materials, energy prices, shipping / freight indexes, MEPS steel price benchmarks, Kallanish, CBAM exposure, financing / working-capital cost.

    Calculation

    Supplier price + transport + insurance + terminal / customs + inland haulage + duties + quota risk + FX impact + CBAM + financing = TRUE LANDED / REPLACEMENT COST PER TONNE

    Outputs

    • Risk-adjusted true landed cost per tonne, per supplier / origin.
    • Quota-exhaustion exposure per shipment.
    • Trade-remedy and CBAM exposure per shipment.
  4. 04

    Stage 4

    Inventory & demand

    Bring in the company’s own product master, consumption, stock and PO position so the system can reason about when the next order is actually needed.

    Inputs

    • Internal Product master — product, commodity code, target delivered purchase price.
    • Internal Monthly consumption per product.
    • Internal Current stock.
    • Internal Outstanding purchase orders.
    • Internal Supplier lead time, minimum order quantity, supplier performance.

    Calculation

    Stock cover = current stock ÷ monthly consumption

    Outputs

    • Expected stock when the next order arrives.
    • Stock-out risk.
    • Required reorder quantity.
    • Safety-stock requirements per product.
  5. 05

    Stage 5

    Market forecast

    Combine every price and pressure signal into a directional view on where the true landed cost is heading over the next 30, 60 and 90 days.

    Inputs

    • Confirmed Current supplier offers.
    • Confirmed MEPS benchmarks.
    • Confirmed Scrap price movement.
    • Confirmed Energy prices.
    • Confirmed FX.
    • Confirmed Shipping / freight rates.
    • Confirmed Quota utilisation.
    • Confirmed CBAM cost.
    • Inferred Kallanish and supplier-network market intelligence.

    Outputs

    • Forecast Expected landed replacement cost in 30 days.
    • Forecast Expected landed replacement cost in 60 days.
    • Forecast Expected landed replacement cost in 90 days.
    • Forecast Probability of price increases / decreases.
    • Forecast Probability of quota exhaustion before delivery.
  6. 06

    Stage 6

    Buying decision engine

    All prior stages converge here. The engine compares target vs. current vs. forecast prices against stock, lead time and risk to produce a concrete buying action per product.

    Compare

    • Target buy price
    • Current supplier offer
    • Current landed cost
    • Forecast landed replacement cost
    • Monthly consumption
    • Current stock
    • Stock cover
    • Supplier lead time
    • Quota risk
    • Supplier reliability
    • Origin-country risk

    Recommended actions

    BUY NOW WAIT NEGOTIATE REQUEST MORE QUOTES SWITCH SUPPLIER SWITCH ORIGIN COUNTRY BUY SMALLER QUANTITY BUY LARGER QUANTITY ACCELERATE DELIVERY
  7. 07

    Stage 7

    Final output

    A single per-product view that a buyer or general manager can act on without opening any other system.

    Per-product display

    • Likely supplier shortlist
    • Best source country
    • Current supplier quote
    • Target buy price
    • True landed cost per tonne
    • 30 / 60 / 90-day replacement-cost forecast
    • Current stock
    • Monthly consumption
    • Stock cover
    • Quota exposure
    • Recommended order quantity
    • Recommended buying date
    • Estimated saving
    • Evidence / confidence score

Commercial objective

Minimise procurement and landed cost while maintaining sufficient stock and avoiding unnecessary quota, duty, FX, freight and supplier risk.

The flowchart is deliberately explicit about what is confirmed data, what is inferred, what is forecast and what is internal — because every downstream recommendation needs to carry its evidence provenance so a buyer can trust or override it.

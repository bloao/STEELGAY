Commercial channel · Digital sourcing

Online B2B marketplace

Platforms for supplier discovery, price comparison and smaller or spot purchases. Useful as a starting point — but not a substitute for real counterparty and mill verification.

Best suited to

Supplier discovery, price comparison, smaller or spot purchases.

Typical transit

Determined by the chosen physical mode.

Cost characteristics

Platform and payment cost plus normal freight and customs.

Main steel risk

Counterparty, mill origin, quality and sanctions verification — the listing itself is not proof of compliance.

Step by step

  1. Use the platform to discover potential mills or traders rather than treating the listing itself as proof of compliance.
  2. Verify legal identity, mill, production location, technical specifications and quality certification.
  3. Establish the real country of origin.
  4. Screen the supplier and underlying material against sanctions.
  5. Obtain sufficient specifications to determine the UK commodity code.
  6. Check duty, quota and trade-remedy exposure.
  7. Agree who acts as the importer and the applicable Incoterm.
  8. Arrange secure commercial payment and the chosen freight mode.
  9. Obtain the same invoice, packing list, transport and origin documents required for an offline transaction.
  10. Submit the CDS declaration and quota claim.
  11. Clear customs, account for VAT and duties and inspect the received goods.

Along the journey — cost & liability

Every variable to price and every point at which contractual liability transfers, arranged in the order they arise on this route.

  1. Discovery & verification

    The platform surfaces the option; the buyer must still verify counterparty and material.

    • Platform / subscription cost. Membership, listing or search-tool fees.
    • Due-diligence cost. Counterparty verification, mill audit and reference-checking.
    • Sanctions & sanctions-adjacent screening. Especially important where the platform aggregates suppliers globally.
    • Specification tightening. Cost of ensuring the online spec sheet matches actual mill capability.

  2. Purchase & payment

    Order placement, escrow and contract terms conducted through the platform.

    • Platform commission. Percentage of order value taken by the marketplace.
    • Payment processing. Escrow, wire or card fees on top of the invoiced price.
    • Currency & FX. Purchase currency, hedging and card foreign-exchange margin.
    • Platform terms of sale. Whether the platform, seller or buyer is liable in a dispute.
    • Incoterm as displayed. Often EXW or FOB by default — the buyer picks up subsequent cost and risk.

  3. Physical & customs

    The chosen physical mode — sea, road, rail or air — runs as normal.

    • Main-carriage freight. Ocean, road, rail or air cost depending on the arrangement.
    • Cargo insurance. Arranged directly by the buyer or via the platform if offered.
    • Terminal handling. OTHC and DTHC at both ends.
    • Customs brokerage, duty, steel measure & VAT. Filed and paid as for any other import.

  4. Post-clearance & delivery

    From UK release to the buyer’s site, plus working capital and audit.

    • UK inland freight. Truck or intermodal from the UK entry point to the buyer’s site.
    • Storage, demurrage & detention. Time-based charges if the container, trailer or berth is not released promptly.
    • Financing / working capital. Cost of tying up cash between payment to the seller and sale or consumption.
    • Quality inspection at receipt. Third-party or in-house quality check on arrival.
    • Record retention. Declaration, quota confirmation, origin evidence and commercial records for the statutory audit period.

Key documents

  • Platform contract / order confirmation. Records the buyer, seller, price and terms.
  • Commercial invoice & packing list. Standard commercial paperwork from the underlying seller.
  • Transport document. Bill of lading, CMR, air waybill or rail consignment depending on mode.
  • Origin & sanctions evidence. Retained to support preferential treatment and third-country processing checks.
  • CDS import declaration. With quota order number where a claim is made.
